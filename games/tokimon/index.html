Tokimon

Sept. 14, 2026

Tokimon is a card battling game where players describe a card - giving it a name, freeform description, and personality - to an LLM, which then generates the card, giving it art, a more thorough description, moves, and more. Players can then battle their cards against each other, with the LLM interpreting the battle outcome, creating a narrative and illustration of the result.

For example, this card was generated from the description “Aero, the Whispering Zephyr: A sentient breeze that carries secrets across kingdoms and slips through the tightest defenses.”

Then, an adversary is generated with the description: “Zeppelin, the Cloud Fortress: A massive, hydrogen-filled warship that rains down explosive damage from above, safe from melee attacks.”

These cards are battled, with Aero winning - the battle is illustrated in the LLM output below.

Players can continue battling the same cards or new ones, racking up points from their victories.

Unfortunately, the game isn't live at the moment, due to the cost of the LLM tokens required to play it. Reach out to me if you're interested in a demo.
